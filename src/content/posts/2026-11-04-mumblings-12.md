It’s been a while. Mumblings is back.

Contentful has been acquired by Salesforce, which means I’m now a Senior Technical Member of Staff (SMTS). Mega news. I’m super excited by it. It’s scary because it’s a new role, new world, but I’m ready to take a knuckle on it.

In previous roles, I’m used to being a very wide T-shaped person across multiple levels, managing as well as technically leading. So this will be interesting to see if I can continue this pace and work my way up through the world of Salesforce.

## Life

I got a dog. Meet Maple. Princess Maple, we call her. She has very much taken full control of my life, and everything in it, with zero regrets. I’ve loved every moment of it, and a side effect of that is my free time has pretty much been nonexistent, which is a bit of a downside, but you move on. Responsibilities.

Having a dog requires a strict routine. I don’t remember the last time I slept past 7:00 a.m., which has been tough, I won’t lie, but I feel like it’s built me to be a better person anyway. Seeing her develop a personality has been the best. Watching her unravel into her own being, with her own moods and her own life, is one of the best things in the world, in my opinion. I would not change it for anything.

Stealing socks. Socks go missing. She disappears into the ether, and you spend the next 20 minutes chasing her to get that sock back. Innocent fun. A headache as well, but I have zero regret.

On the upside, I’m getting way more touching-the-grass moments. In the age of sitting behind a desk consistently, constantly, touching grass has been massively appreciated. I’ve not been present in the AI sphere at the moment, which has been quite nice, but also massive FOMO on all the latest stuff. I mean, I use AI for work every day, but I’m talking about on a personal level. That’s mainly because I’ve been outdoors more, which has been really nice, actually.

### Fitness

In the world of fitness, there’s not a major update from me, but my partner ran her first ever half marathon. I’ve seen her train for months leading up to this, overcoming injuries, overcoming getting a puppy and that lifestyle change, and still going out and being an inspiration by running this half marathon. Mega props to her. It’s been amazing to see her come this far.

I took the pup to watch, which was a challenge in itself, taking a small puppy to an event like this. Making her experience things like the London Tube has definitely been quite fun.

### Games and books

My time has been massively taken by the game Ace Combat at the moment. I love a good arcade shooter like this. It kind of makes you forget about the worries of the world. I’ve always been a lover of airplanes, and Ace Combat 2 holds a very close space in my heart and in my mind. So playing all those years later, nearly 30 years later, is great. It’s fun.

My primary focus as of late has been reading loads, so my bookshelf on this website has just been massively updated. I’ve read the Mistborn first series - Mistborn 1, 2, and 3. Amazing, really loved. Amazing writing. It unraveled me. I was obsessed.

I’ve also read all of Dungeon Crawler Carl. All of them have been amazing, and I’ve loved them all. Dungeon Crawler Carl has been my running fitness sort of book. Mistborn was what I was reading before bed, and now I’m reading Red Rising. I’m very book-minded at the moment.
