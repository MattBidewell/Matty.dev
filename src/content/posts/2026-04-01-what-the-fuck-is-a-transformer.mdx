Before a transformer can do anything with a sentence, it has to break that
sentence into pieces it can actually work with. Those pieces are called
**tokens** — not quite words, not quite characters, just whatever chunks the
model's vocabulary happens to carve the text into.

Watch what happens when the sentence below gets tokenized:

<TokenVisualizer text="the cat sat on the mat" />

Each of those chunks becomes a vector, and the rest of the paper is really
just describing what happens to those vectors next.
